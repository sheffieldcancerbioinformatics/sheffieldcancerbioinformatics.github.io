Tamara is a cancer researcher with experience working on both colorectal and breast cancer projects, with a strong focus on omics approaches including proteomics, epigenomics, transcriptomics, and genomics. She is currently pursuing a PhD at the University of Sheffield under the supervision of Professor Penny Ottewell in collaboration with Dr Quayle. Her research focuses on breast cancer dormancy and bone metastasis, aiming to better understand mechanisms driving metastatic progression and therapeutic resistance.

Beyond her core research, Tamara has a broader interest in emerging biomedical technologies, including gene editing and CRISPR-Cas systems, as well as longevity and ageing research.

## Professional Experience

### **PhD Candidate | University of Sheffield**
*2025 - Present*

- Research focused on breast cancer dormancy and bone metastasis.
- Utilising a combination of experimental and computational approaches, including histology, immunohistochemistry, single-nucleus RNA sequencing (snRNA-seq), and 2D cell culture models.
