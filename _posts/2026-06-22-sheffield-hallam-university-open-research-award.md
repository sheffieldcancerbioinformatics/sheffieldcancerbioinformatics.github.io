We are delighted to announce our PhD project *Unlocking Precision Medicine: Advanced Survival Analysis Tools for Validation of Biomarkers in Cancer* has been awarded the Sheffield Hallam University Open Research Award 2026 in recognition of our commitment to embedding open research practices throughout our work.

<div align="center">
	<img src="/images/news/sheffield-hallam-university-open-research-award.png" width="auto" width="75%"/>
</div>
<br>
The project is aimed at developing new and reproducible approaches for validating cancer biomarkers using survival models. By combining openly available clinical and transcriptomic datasets with transparent analytical workflows, version-controlled software, and accessible research tools, the project aims to improve the robustness, reproducibility, and accessibility of survival analysis in cancer research. 

A central focus of the project is ensuring that the outputs are available to the wider research community. Alongside peer-reviewed publications, we are developing open-source R packages, harmonised tumour compendia, and a browser-based application that will enable researchers and clinicians to apply advanced survival analysis methods without requiring programming expertise.  

We are grateful to Sheffield Hallam University for recognising this work and for its continued support of open research.
